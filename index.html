By standardizing on three universal domains, you create a structured architecture for evaluating leadership. The Blind-Spot Lens: Self-Report vs. External Reality Self-report tools measure identity (who leaders think they are / want to be seen as), while 360s, ONA, and CPP measure reputation and reality (how they actually process, behave, and connect). Incorporating self-report tools (Hogan, OPQ, Saville Wave, Prophet, GC Index, CliftonStrengths) directly into your 3-Domain Model creates a Perception Delta / Blind-Spot Indicator: Δ=Self-Reported Strength−External Measured Impact Δ>+20 (Self-Delusion / Blind Spot): The leader rates themselves as an agile visionary (e.g., GC Index "Game Changer"), but ONA/360 scores reveal they isolate teams and struggle with ambiguity. Δ<−20 (Latent Capability): The leader understates their strategic influence, but ONA and 360 data show high informal organizational brokerage. How These Columns Are Calculated Each column represents a distinct analytical layer that feeds into the leadership assessment: LIR (Leadership Impact Readiness) — 0 to 100 Score: LIR=Objective Capability Vector×Contextual Role Demand It is not a generic IQ score or a standard 360 average. It measures how well an executive's profile (Cognitive Processing + Behavioral Impact + Network Centrality) fulfills the specific demands of their transformation block. If an executive scores high on execution (85) but is placed in an ambiguity-heavy venture role requiring 90 in strategic flexibility, their LIR drops to reflect that mismatch. ZF % (Zenger Folkman Percentile): The norm-referenced percentile rank of their overall leadership effectiveness based on multi-rater feedback across the 19 core competencies. State (The Recommendation Output): Calculated by subtracting role demand from the candidate's capability score: Δ=LIR Score−Role Demand Target Δ≥−3: Aligned (Role fit is solid; capability matches or exceeds target). −10≤Δ<−3: Stretch (Viable with structured coaching and targeted deputy support). Δ<−10 (or critical Hogan Derailer flagged): Redeploy (Structural mismatch; profile is misallocated relative to role demands). Better Alternatives to "State" "State" reads like raw database syntax. Depending on the framing you want to present to clients or the board, here are stronger alternatives: Proposed Column Label Badge Values Commercial / Advisory Context Role Fit (Recommended) Aligned • Stretch • Mismatch Clear, objective, and executive-ready. Advisory Signal Optimal • Calibrate • Redeploy Emphasizes strategic talent advisory and action. Readiness Ready Now • Developing • Realign Aligns with standard succession planning language. Bench Health Strong Fit • High Stretch • At Risk Useful for ExCo portfolio reviews.
